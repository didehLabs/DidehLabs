# didehLabs

didehLabs is an AI growth intelligence studio for local businesses. Our engine finds the hidden digital leaks that cost owner-run businesses customers and revenue, then our engineers fix them.

## What We Do

- Scan local businesses on Google Maps and business directories and capture their website, contact details and social presence.
- Audit the real website: speed, mobile experience, security, broken assets, booking and contact paths, and trust signals.
- Turn findings into business language (lost bookings, lost trust, lost revenue) and rank each opportunity.
- Reach out to owners by email or WhatsApp with what we found, follow up, and track every deal.
- Fix the leaks: website performance, booking systems, trust signals, local SEO, mobile experience and conversion funnels.

## Who We Help

Owner-run businesses that live on local customers: private clinics, dentists, law firms, accountants, real estate agencies, HVAC, plumbers, electricians, roofing, remodeling, locksmiths, hair salons, fitness studios, bakeries, coffee shops and dog groomers.

Our engine scans markets in the UAE, UK, Canada, Australia, USA and Nigeria.

## Our Approach

- Evidence-first: every recommendation comes from a real audit of a real website.
- Plain language: we talk about lost revenue, not technical scores.
- AI-native: AI finds and explains the gaps. Engineers fix them.

## Services

- Website Performance Fixes
- Booking System Implementation
- Trust Signal Improvements
- Local SEO Visibility
- Mobile Experience Overhauls
- Conversion Funnel Repair

## Technology

Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS for websites. Node.js, TypeScript, Express, Python, Django and PostgreSQL on the back end. Gemini AI, Lighthouse and Playwright power the audit engine. Deployed on GCP and Vercel.
